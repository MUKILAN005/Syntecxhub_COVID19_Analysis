COVID-19 Data Analysis - Summar

1. The analysis compares daily COVID-19 cases across the top 5 countries in the dataset.

2. A 7-day rolling average was used to smooth daily fluctuations and identify the overall case trend.

3. The United States (US) had the highest total confirmed cases among the countries analyzed in this dataset.

4. The peak daily cases for the US were 300,462 on 1/2/21.

5. Higher increases in daily cases indicate faster spread during specific periods. This analysis uses case trends and rolling averages rather than estimating a formal reproduction number (R0 or Rt).

6. The analysis exported country comparison and 7-day rolling average charts for visualization and reporting.
